This fixture selects the wide splash shell but keeps an ordinary page heading. It demonstrates that the splash layout does not imply a hero.

## Wide article content

Article Markdown follows the heading in the same reading order as a documentation page, without either sidebar. The title and article share a centered 45rem width cap; the wider hero region is only used when a hero is present.
